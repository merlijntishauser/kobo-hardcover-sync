# Covers (the Mac app)

Mode: Experience. The reader is inside their own library; the covers lead
from the first viewport, the interface recedes. Same surface in local and
server mode: the tool hands the app the books either way.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The library as a press run. Covers come off the press in three
rows drifting left, the work itself filling the window; below it the
delivery pile, where the book in hand and one tip are laid. It refuses
the category default: a scrolling grid of equal covers with a search
field on top.

OWN-WORLD: The Galley Proof, unchanged. Galley Stock ground, covers with
the cover shadow as the only depth, a 3px double rule between run and
pile, Source Serif 4 for titles only, the line gauge, the marks. Red
caret on a cover the next sync changes; the tip is the blue circled
query, a question to the reader. No pills, no glow, no blur.

STORY: The reader sees their reading at a glance, front row recent,
middle row the rest read, back row never opened. A hover stops the run
and lifts one cover; a click opens its Details; space shows it large.
The pile says what they are reading and asks one question.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Run in the top 62%: back row 72pt covers, middle 108pt,
front 150pt, each slower toward the back. Double rule. Pile in the
bottom 38%: the current book at 150pt, the tip at 150pt beside it, then
the title in Source Serif at 22pt, the gauge, the facts, the tip's
question with Open and Not now.

FORM: The press run, fifth on my ordered list of seven, dealt as the lead
of seed f11c963c (surface, experience); code-led, no image generation.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Approved by the reader

- Locked 2026-10-26 from three dealt structures (press run, reading year,
  shelf). Locking it approves a second authored motion for this surface
  only: the drift and the lift. Under reduced motion the run stands still
  and scrolls by hand.
